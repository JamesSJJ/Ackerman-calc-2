Calculation assumptions and limitations

Tie-rod lengths are established at zero steering input and remain constant. The plate rotates rigidly about the stem; knuckle tie-rod holes rotate about their respective projected steering pivots. Wheel heading is assumed aligned with the ATV centerline at zero input. Solver uses a continuous nearest-angle root. Ideal Ackermann uses wheelbase and front wheel-center track. The displayed percentage compares the actual inside/outside angle difference with ideal at the same inside angle (100% ideal, 0% parallel). At zero turn or if linkage cannot solve, the result is undefined. This 2D tool excludes caster, KPI, bump steer, compliance and actual three-dimensional steering-axis projection. Verify all geometry and mechanical clearances before modifying a vehicle.
